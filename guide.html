FOR COMPANY ADMINISTRATORS

Set up your company in one sitting.

01

Register your company

Contact Novature to register your company. You receive a Company Admin login and a 7-day free trial starts. Sign in at the web app – a computer is easiest for setup.

02

Open the Menu

Everything is in the Menu button at the bottom-left corner. Sections are grouped: People, Attendance, Leave, Payroll, Reports and Settings.

03

Follow the setup checklist

The Setup checklist walks you through company details and logo, departments and designations, shifts, holidays, leave policy, payroll settings and work locations (QR gate / GPS geofence).

04

Upload employees with CSV check

Download the CSV template, fill in your employees and upload it. The CSV check lists any missing or wrong values row by row before anything is saved, so you fix errors first.

05

Review the dashboard

Once employees are loaded, the dashboard shows headcount, attendance today and pending items.

06

Approvals and reports

Managers approve leave and requests; HR sees all approvals and runs attendance, leave and payroll reports.

07

Invite employees

Share the download page with employees. They install the Android app or use the web app and sign in with the login you created.

Subscription

After the 7-day free trial: ₹10 per active employee per month (plus applicable taxes). Subscriptions are handled on novature.in only.

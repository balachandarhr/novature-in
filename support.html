SUPPORT QUESTIONS

A few practical answers.

How do I request a demo?

Submit a request using the contact form, or contact us through WhatsApp.

How are website enquiries delivered?

The form sends enquiries through FormSubmit to info@novature.in once the recipient mailbox has completed its one-time activation. A spam-protection check may appear. WhatsApp opens a direct chat; SMS alerts are not enabled.

Can Novature be configured for our company?

Company requirements and supported configuration are reviewed during discovery. Any custom development or integration must be confirmed in the scope.

What are the support hours and response times?

Support channels, hours and response commitments will be specified in your customer agreement. This website does not promise a 24/7 service or a fixed response time.

Can I share employee data in a support request?

Share only what is necessary to explain the issue. Never share passwords, authentication codes or full sensitive employee records through an ordinary enquiry.

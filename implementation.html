IMPLEMENTATION

A considered rollout.
A confident start.

Begin with understanding. Define the scope. Prepare your data and your users. A practical implementation plan makes the transition easier to manage.

01

Discovery & product walkthrough

Share your workforce size, operating patterns and current HR challenges. Review the product before choosing the scope.

  • Industry and company structure
  • Priority workflows
  • Module readiness and product fit
02

Scope & commercial proposal

Agree what the implementation will cover and the responsibilities on both sides.

  • Selected modules and configuration
  • Data and onboarding requirements
  • Pricing, support terms and rollout approach
03

Data preparation & configuration

Prepare the information needed to establish your HR workflows.

  • Employee master and reporting structure
  • Shift, leave and holiday information
  • Payroll inputs and policy review
04

Validation & onboarding

Review representative scenarios with your HR team before using the application in daily operations.

  • Attendance and approval scenarios
  • Employee and manager orientation
  • Payroll settings and reports where in scope
05

Go-live & support

Move to day-to-day use with agreed support channels and responsibilities.

  • Agreed launch scope
  • Issue reporting and follow-up
  • Ongoing requirements review

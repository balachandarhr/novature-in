ABOUT THE PRODUCT

Novature HRMS, built in India.

Novature HRMS is a corporate HR system for small and mid-sized companies: attendance with QR gate scan and GPS geofence, leave and two-level approvals, payroll with PF/ESI/PT and payslips, employee self-service, reports and a guided company setup with CSV checks. Each company’s data is kept separate. Companies register on novature.in for a 7-day free trial; employees sign in with the Android app or the web app.

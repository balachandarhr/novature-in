Privacy Policy

How the Novature HRMS Android app, web app and website handle personal data.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Novature HRMS (the Android app, the web app and the website, operated by Novature, India – “we”, “us”) handles personal data. Novature HRMS is a business HR system. Your employer (the “Company”) subscribes to Novature HRMS and decides what employee data is entered. For employee data, the Company is the data controller and Novature processes the data on the Company’s behalf.

1. Who can use the app

The app is for employees, managers and HR administrators of companies that have a Novature HRMS account. Accounts are created by the Company administrator or through company registration on our website. The app is not directed at children.

We do not use advertising SDKs, do not sell personal data and do not use data for advertising.

3. Who can see the data

Data is separated per company. Users of one company cannot see another company’s data. Within a company, access depends on role (employee, manager, HR/Company admin). Novature platform administrators can access company-level account and billing information to operate the service, and may access other data only to provide support or when legally required.

4. Service providers

We use Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) to host the service, GitHub Pages to host the website and web app, and Razorpay for website payments. These providers process data only to provide their services to us.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access is restricted by sign-in and per-company, role-based database access rules. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Retention

Data is kept while the Company’s account is active and as long as the Company needs it for HR, payroll and legal record-keeping. When a Company account is closed, data is deleted on request (see Data Deletion), except where we must keep it by law.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and request corrections to your profile in the app. You can deny location or camera permission in Android settings; geo-fenced or QR attendance may then not work. To access, correct or delete your data, contact your Company administrator or write to info@novature.in. Requests are handled in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

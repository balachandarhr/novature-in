INSTALL ON ANDROID

Install in four simple steps.

01

Download the app

Tap Download Android App above. The file NovatureHRMS.apk downloads straight to your phone. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.

02

Allow installation from this source

Open the downloaded file from the notification or the Files / Downloads app. Android asks for permission the first time: tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for Chrome or Files, then press back.

Path on most phones: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps.

03

Install and open

Tap Install. If Google Play Protect shows a warning for an unknown developer, tap More details → Install anyway. When finished, tap Open. The Novature HRMS icon also appears on your home screen.

04

Sign in

Enter the email and password given by your company HR administrator and tap Sign in. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen. Employees see their home screen with attendance, leave, payslips and announcements.

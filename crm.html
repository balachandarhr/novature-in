01

Download the app

Tap Download Android App above. The file Novature_CRM.apk downloads to your phone. If Chrome asks whether to keep the file, tap Download anyway.

02

Allow installation from this source

Open the downloaded file from the notification or the Files / Downloads app. The first time, Android asks for permission: tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source for Chrome or Files, then press back.

Path on most phones: Settings → Apps → Special app access → Install unknown apps.

03

Tap Install. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap More details → Install anyway. Open the app and sign in with the email and password given by your Company Admin. You can change your password any time from Change password in the app.

WHAT THE MARKETING TEAM CAN DO

From the first enquiry
to monthly sales figures.

01

Dashboard and targets

See year-to-date and last-month figures against each salesperson’s target. Company leaders set monthly targets; the team sees the same units everywhere — ₹ lakhs and tons.

02

Enquiries, visits and samples

Salespeople log new enquiries, daily visit reports and sample collections. They can add a new customer name on the spot; a data-entry user reviews and corrects names later.

03

Sales upload and analysis

The Company Admin can import and export customers, sales and targets at any time. Uploaded or imported figures show on the dashboard and sales analysis immediately.

04

Access that fits the team

The company’s admin decides who can sign in, within the paid seats. After the company is on a trial or a paid subscription, every seated employee can use the app.

ROLES

The right view
for each login.

Company Admin

Full control: users, seats, customer-name review and merge, targets, import/export and every report.

Manager

Team view of figures and sales analysis, without changing another person’s records.

Sales person

Own customers, enquiries, visits and samples, plus their own target and actual.

Data entry / uploader

Enter enquiries, visits and sales files on behalf of the team, and tidy customer names.

Accounts

All sales figures and collections, without marketing-only work.
